Built-in modules

Everything a music-loving server needs.

One bot, every feature. Configure DJ roles, filters, and leaderboards from the dashboard — no restarts needed.

High-Quality Streaming

Crystal-clear audio up to 320kbps. Supports YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, direct URLs and playlist links — just paste and play.

Smart Queue

Add, remove, shuffle, move or loop individual tracks. View the full queue at a glance — with track duration, requester and progress.

Saved Playlists

Save your current queue as a named playlist and load it back in seconds. Per-server and per-user playlists, shareable with the whole community.

Listener Leaderboard Soon

XP earned per minute of listening. Weekly and all-time boards with /music-top — the more your members vibe, the higher they rank.

Audio Filters & Effects

Bass boost, nightcore, vaporwave, 8D, karaoke, soft, and more — toggle in real time with /filter. Stack multiple effects at once.

Live Lyrics

Get synced lyrics for any currently playing track with /lyrics. Displays verse-by-verse so your server can sing along in chat.

DJ Role Controls

Assign a DJ role to give fine-grained control. Regular members can request; DJs can skip, clear, filter and manage the full queue.

24/7 Mode

Keep Tempo in the voice channel around the clock — even when everyone leaves. Toggle with /247 and it never disconnects.

Web Dashboard Soon

Manage DJ roles, default volume, filter presets, playlist libraries and leaderboard settings — all from a beautiful web dashboard.

Slash Commands

Every command at a glance.

Type / in Discord — autocomplete, descriptions, and permission hints built in.

/play

Play a song by name, URL, or playlist link from any source.

/pause

Pause the current track. /resume to continue.

/skip

Skip the current track and jump to the next in queue.

/queue

Show the full queue — titles, duration, and who requested each.

/nowplaying

Beautiful now-playing card with progress bar and cover art.

/volume

Set playback volume from 0 – 200%.

/loop

Loop the current track or the entire queue.

/shuffle

Randomise the order of tracks in the queue.

/remove

Remove a specific track from the queue by its position.

/move

Move a track to a different position in the queue.

/filter

Apply or clear audio filters — bassboost, nightcore, 8D, and more.

/lyrics

Fetch synced lyrics for the currently playing song.

/playlist save

Save your current queue as a named playlist.

/playlist load

Load a previously saved playlist into the queue.

/playlist list

View all saved playlists for your server.

/247

Toggle 24/7 mode — Tempo stays in VC indefinitely.

/music-top

Show the listener leaderboard — weekly or all-time.

/stop

Stop playback and clear the queue entirely.
