01 Who We Are

Byte Labs ("we", "us", "our") is the operator of the Byte Labs Discord bot platform, including SP4RK, Server Official, Wave, and the Byte Labs web dashboard. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

When you use our Service, you are placing your trust in us to handle your information responsibly. We take that responsibility seriously.

02 Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide and improve the Service. Here is a breakdown:

Category Data Collected Source
Discord Account User ID, username, avatar, email address (if granted) Discord OAuth2 login
Server Data Guild IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, server settings Bot permissions & dashboard configuration
Moderation Logs Warned/banned user IDs, message content flagged by automod Bot activity in your server
Bot Request Forms Name, email, Discord handle, bot description Submitted via the Byte Labs website
Feedback Name (optional), rating, comment Public feedback form on our landing page
Session Data Encrypted session cookie (contains Discord user ID and login state) Set automatically on login
Usage Logs Dashboard actions (non-personal, e.g. "AutoMod settings updated") Internal audit logging

We do not collect: your passwords, payment card details (handled entirely by third-party processors), or private Discord messages.

03 How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication — Verifying your identity via Discord OAuth2 to grant access to the dashboard.
  • Bot functionality — Storing server settings, moderation rules, and user configurations so the bots work correctly.
  • Moderation — Logging warnings, bans, and automod decisions to give server admins an audit trail.
  • Service improvement — Analysing aggregate, anonymised usage patterns to improve features.
  • Support — Responding to bot requests or feedback submissions you send us.
  • Security — Detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes, ever.

04 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database. Supabase uses industry-standard encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Configuration data is also cached locally on the server for resilience.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS-only access enforced on all endpoints.
  • HTTP security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, etc.).
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax.
  • Secrets and API keys are stored as environment variables, never in source code.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

05 Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Server configuration — Retained for as long as the bot is active on your server. Removed upon request or when the bot is kicked.
  • Moderation logs — Retained for up to 12 months, then automatically purged.
  • Session data — Expires after 30 days of inactivity.
  • Feedback submissions — Retained indefinitely in aggregate form unless deletion is requested.
  • Bot request submissions — Retained for 6 months, then purged.

06 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Discord (discord.com) — Identity provider and bot platform. Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase (supabase.com) — Database and backend infrastructure. Data is stored in Supabase's managed Postgres.
  • Google Gemini AI (ai.google.dev) — Processes message content for AI-powered automod. Content sent to Gemini is subject to Google's AI Privacy Policy. Only messages that trigger automod review are sent.
  • Resend (resend.com) — Used to send transactional emails (e.g. bot request confirmations). Only your email and name are transmitted.

We have agreements in place with these providers requiring them to handle data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

07 Cookies

We use a minimal number of cookies, all of which are functional and necessary:

  • Session cookie (session) — Stores your login state after Discord OAuth. HttpOnly, Secure. Expires after 30 days.
  • Cookie consent (bl_cookie_consent) — Records whether you have acknowledged the cookie notice. 1-year expiry.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. You can block all cookies in your browser settings, but this will prevent dashboard login from working.

08 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to Rectification Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
Right to Erasure Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
Right to Object Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.
Right to Portability Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Right to Restriction Request that we limit how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days. Note that some data cannot be deleted if it is required for legal compliance or security purposes.

EU/EEA Users (GDPR): You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have not been respected.

09 Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data without parental consent, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify active users.

Your continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
