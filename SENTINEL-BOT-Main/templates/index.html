Automod (Auto-Action)

When ON, SP4RK automatically mutes / tempbans / kicks members based on their warning count. When OFF, nothing changes — warnings still get logged to staff but no automatic punishment is applied. Owners, staff and equal/higher-role members are never affected.

Support Tickets

Private 1-on-1 channels between members and your staff team. Set the Ticket Category ID in General Settings so new tickets are created in the right place.

How tickets work
  • Members open one with /ticket <reason> in any channel.
  • The bot creates a private channel under your configured Ticket Category, visible only to that member and your staff roles.
  • Staff close the ticket with /ticket-close or the close button — the channel is archived and a transcript is saved.
