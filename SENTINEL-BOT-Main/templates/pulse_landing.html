Built-in modules

Every insight your server actually needs.

One bot. Zero dashboards to set up. Ask for stats in chat and get beautiful embeds back in seconds.

Member Growth Tracking

Daily join/leave log, weekly net growth, and all-time member count history. See exactly when your community surged — or dipped.

Message Activity

Per-channel and per-user message counts for today, this week, or all-time. Spot your most active channels and quiet ones at a glance.

Top Contributors

Leaderboard of your most active members by messages, voice time, or reactions. Recognise who keeps your server alive.

Voice Engagement

Minutes spent in voice channels per member. Weekly voice leaderboards so your community knows who the real voice-chat regulars are.

Channel Health

Rank channels by activity, find dead channels worth archiving, and spot which ones deserve more attention — all from /channelstats.

Slash Commands

Ask for any insight right in Discord.

Every report is a slash command — no dashboard trip needed for day-to-day analytics.

/stats

Full server overview — members, online, channels, roles, boosts, and message totals.

/activity

Message count breakdown per channel for today, this week, or all-time.

/top

Top members leaderboard — by messages, voice time, or reactions. Paginated.

/growth

Member join/leave counts and net growth for the past 7, 30, or 90 days.

/voicetime

Voice activity leaderboard — who has spent the most time in VCs this week.

/channelstats

Ranked channel activity list — most to least active, with message counts.

/memberstats

Detailed breakdown for a specific member — messages, voice time, join date.

/report

Generate a full weekly summary embed on demand for any period.

/heatmap

Hour-of-day activity heatmap in text-art form right in Discord.

/pulse-help

Show the full Pulse command list with descriptions.
